Automatic Narration Segment boundaries

Question: how should the Narration Application create Narration Segments from EPUB chapters, headings, paragraphs, and text length — and when may the Narrator accept, join, or split a boundary without making import feel technical?

Book parts after import

Pick a sample book. The pure segmentation rules run once. Then try join, split, and accept. Watch whether the parts feel right to record.

Proposed automatic rules

These rules are the thing under test. Change them only if a walkthrough feels wrong.

  • A new chapter always starts a new Narration Segment.
  • A major heading inside a chapter always starts a new Narration Segment.
  • Paragraphs pack into the current part until the next paragraph would push the part past about 180 words.
  • A paragraph never splits in the middle.
  • A leftover shorter than about 40 words joins the previous part in the same section.
  • Opening credits, chapters, and closing credits stay separate sections.

Proposed product decision to react to

  1. Import stays quiet. After the Narrator chooses an EPUB file, the Narration Application creates Narration Segments automatically and shows only “Your book is ready.” No boundary editor on the happy path.
  2. Accept is the default. Opening the Project accepts the automatic parts. The Narrator does not confirm each boundary.
  3. Join and split stay secondary. During recording or review, the current part offers “Combine with next part” and, when a part holds more than one paragraph, “Split after this paragraph.”
  4. No technical language. Screens say “part,” not “segment,” “boundary,” “heading level,” or word counts as requirements. Word counts may appear as quiet help only.
  5. Export still follows chapters. Platform Export files follow chapter or section structure from the book, not every small Narration Segment. Small parts only shape recording work.
